Method and limitations. This follows the interpretation discussed in the chat: 1/14 base team chance, plus 4% of the base chance for each player after the second, with each death removing 10% of one player's expected share of the 700 available contribution points. The adjusted team chance is assumed proportional to remaining contribution. Jagex has not published a complete death-to-purple formula, so death-adjusted figures are estimates, not verified exact in-game odds. It also assumes a standard successful raid with otherwise full team contribution. Results may change with game updates. Official TFA loot mechanics post.
